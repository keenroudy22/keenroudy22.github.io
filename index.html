The first roster

Built in Indiana.
Raised around teams.

I grew up in Indiana, where sports made teamwork less of a value statement and more of a habit. You learn to do your job, communicate early, and keep moving when the plan changes.

My brothers, Koby and KJ, were part of that first roster. The lessons stuck: show up, look out for your people, and leave things better than you found them.

Most of my career has been some version of the same assignment: get the right people lined up, make the complicated part understandable, and finish the job.

Flagship build

MyGolfLinks

Where people, process, product, and golf finally met.

MyGolfLinks is a golf technology platform built for players, courses, and partners. I co-founded it because golf has plenty of technology—but too much of it makes the people using it work around the software.

My role lives between an idea and a dependable release. I help turn workflows into requirements, plan permissions and data paths, coordinate development and testing, document what breaks, and keep the next priority clear.
